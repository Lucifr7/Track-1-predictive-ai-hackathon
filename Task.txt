2. TASK 2: COST MODEL & ASSUMPTIONS (IN MALAYSIAN RINGGIT - RM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* All figures are clearly documented engineering assumptions based on Malaysian
  SME machining industry averages.

A. Baseline Operational Rates:
- Technician Labor Cost: RM 30 / hour (based on ~RM 3,500 - RM 4,500/month salary + overhead)
- CNC Machine Downtime Cost: RM 300 / hour (lost output value, idle operator, schedule delays)
- False Alarm Inspection Cost: RM 15 (20–30 minutes of technician inspection time finding no defect)

B. Cost Breakdown per Failure Mode:
1. Tool Wear Failure (TWF):
   - Planned Fix (RM 100):
     Swap dull insert before shift starts (15 min labor + RM 80 cutting insert).
   - Unexpected Breakdown (RM 650):
     Tool snaps mid-cut. Scraps workpiece, cleans debris, resets tool and offsets
     (~1.5 hrs downtime @ RM 450 + RM 120 scrapped part + RM 80 new tool).

2. Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF):
   - Planned Fix (RM 70):
     Top up coolant, clean chip filters, inspect fan (30 min labor + RM 50 fluid).
   - Unexpected Breakdown (RM 850):
     Thermal trip shuts down machine mid-cycle; machine cool-down, thermal inspection,
     and recalibration (~2.5 hrs downtime @ RM 750 + RM 100 fluid flush & labor).

3. Power Failure (PWF):
   - Planned Fix (RM 50):
     Inspect electrical terminals, belt tension, and spindle drive load (30 min labor).
   - Unexpected Breakdown (RM 700):
     Spindle motor trips breaker or inverter overload mid-heavy cut; electrical reset,
     re-homing machine (~2 hrs downtime @ RM 600 + RM 100 diagnosis).

4. Overstrain Failure (OSF):
   - Planned Fix (RM 100):
     Worn tool running high torque; replace insert and adjust feed rate (20 min labor + RM 80 insert).
   - Unexpected Breakdown (RM 1,400):
     High torque causes blunt tool to bind, break, and jam workpiece; potential spindle
     misalignment (~3.5 hrs downtime @ RM 1,050 + RM 250 scrapped part + RM 100 clamp/insert).

5. False Alarm (Inspection of healthy machine):
   - Cost: RM 15 (30 minutes of technician labor finding no issue).

C. Dataset Weighted Averages (based on ai4i2020 distribution: HDF 32.5%, OSF 27.7%, PWF 26.8%, TWF 13.0%):
- Average Unexpected Breakdown Cost (C_fail): ~RM 936
- Average Planned Fix Cost (C_fix): ~RM 77
- Net Savings per Caught Failure (C_fail - C_fix): ~RM 859

3. TASK 3: EXPLANATIONS & RECOMMENDED ACTIONS (TECHNICIAN PLAYBOOK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designed for clear shop-floor action under the "Explain" judging criterion:

1. Tool Wear Failure (TWF):
   - Explanation:
     "Tool nearing end of life: Cumulative cutting time has exceeded 200 minutes,
      indicating a dull or degraded cutting insert."
   - Recommended Action:
     "Inspect the cutting insert for edge wear or micro-chipping; replace with a fresh
      insert before starting the shift."

2. Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF):
   - Explanation:
     "Insufficient cooling: The machine is running slowly and heat is not dissipating
      properly between the workpiece and ambient air (temperature difference < 8.6 K)."
   - Recommended Action:
     "Check coolant fluid levels and nozzle direction; inspect radiator cooling fans
      for metal chip buildup or air blockage."

3. Power Failure (PWF):
   - Explanation:
     "Spindle power out of safe range: Machine power consumption is outside normal
      operating limits (<3.5 kW or >9.0 kW), risking a motor stall or electrical trip."
   - Recommended Action:
     "Check spindle drive belts for slipping or wear, verify electrical supply voltage,
      and ensure spindle bearings rotate freely."

4. Overstrain Failure (OSF):
   - Explanation:
     "High mechanical strain: Heavy cutting torque combined with a worn tool is putting
      excessive mechanical load on the machine spindle."
   - Recommended Action:
     "Replace the cutting insert immediately and verify programmed feed-rate settings
      before resuming heavy cutting cycles."

4. TASK 4: BUSINESS IMPACT & ROI FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Formula for Judges:
   Net Monthly Savings = [N_fail * Recall * (C_fail - C_fix)] - [False_Alarms * C_false]

   Where:
   - N_fail = Total machine breakdowns occurring per month without predictive AI
   - Recall = Model's sensitivity (% of actual failures detected early)
   - Precision = Model's accuracy of positive alarms
   - False_Alarms = (N_fail * Recall) * [(1 - Precision) / Precision]
   - C_fail = RM 936 (weighted average breakdown cost)
   - C_fix = RM 77 (weighted average planned fix cost)
   - C_false = RM 15 (technician inspection cost for false alarm)

B. Worked Example & Actual Model Verification (50-Machine CNC Factory):
   - Scenario: 50 CNC machines, 15 breakdowns/month baseline
   - Actual Model Performance (on Test Set via train.py):
     * Recall:    85.29% (Catches 58 of 68 failures)
     * Precision: 63.04%
     * ROC-AUC:   0.9852
   1. Breakdowns Prevented: 15 * 85.29% = 12.8 machines/month
   2. Missed Breakdowns: 15 - 12.8 = 2.2 machines/month
   3. Gross Repair & Downtime Savings: 12.8 * RM 859 = RM 10,990.15 / month
   4. False Alarms Generated: 12.8 * [(1 - 0.6304) / 0.6304] = 7.5 inspections/month
   5. Penalty for False Alarms: 7.5 * RM 15 = RM 112.50 / month
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   >>> NET MONTHLY SAVINGS: RM 10,877.65 / month
   >>> NET ANNUAL SAVINGS:  RM 130,531.76 / year
